B. Home Security 家居安全

  1. What happens in this house stays in this house. Do not share our matters with outsiders. 家事不外傳。不要與外人談及我們的事情。
  2. Do not leave the baby alone, no matter how short the time is. 無論時間多短，都不可讓寶寶獨自一人。
  3. Do not bring anyone home. 不得帶任何人回家。
  4. You must not smoke or drink alcohol. 不得吸煙或飲酒。
  5. On holidays, return home before 20:30. You cannot stay outside overnight. 假期須於晚上 8 時 30 分前回家，不可在外過夜。

C. Baby Care 照顧寶寶

  1. Wear clean, comfortable clothing suitable for child care. Wash hands with soap before caring for the baby. 穿著乾淨、舒適及適合照顧幼兒的衣服。照顧寶寶前先用肥皂洗手。
  2. Ensure all feeding equipment is sterilised before each use. 確保所有餵食用具每次使用前已消毒。
  3. Monitor the baby for any food allergies or indigestion. 留意寶寶有否食物過敏或消化不良。
  4. Keep the baby's bed safe and clean. Make sure the baby is sleeping on his back. 保持寶寶的床安全清潔，確保寶寶仰睡。
  5. Change diapers promptly to prevent rashes and discomfort. Dispose of diapers in a sanitary manner. 及時更換尿片，以防紅疹及不適。以衞生方式棄置尿片。
  6. Bathe the baby daily with baby-safe products. Never leave the baby unattended during bath. 每天用嬰兒專用產品為寶寶洗澡。洗澡時絕不可讓寶寶無人看管。
  7. Monitor the baby's health and report any symptoms of illness immediately. Give medication only as directed by us. 留意寶寶健康狀況，如有任何病徵請立即報告。只可按我們的指示給藥。
  8. Keep the environment free from hazards (e.g. sharp objects, small items that can be swallowed). 保持環境沒有危險物品（如尖銳物品、可吞下的小物件）。

D. Cellphone 手提電話

  1. Minimise the use of cellphone, especially if you are the only adult with the baby, no matter inside or outside the house. 盡量減少使用手機，尤其當你是唯一與寶寶在一起的成人時，無論在家或外出。
  2. If you need extra mobile data, you need to buy it from your own pocket. 如需額外流動數據，須自費購買。
  3. You can use our home WiFi after finishing your work. 完成工作後可使用家中 WiFi。

E. Electrical Appliances 電器

  1. Air conditioners may be used when it is higher than 24°C, or if you have a pretty good reason. 氣溫高於 24°C 或有充分理由時，可開冷氣。
  2. Only turn on one air conditioner at a time when you are alone at home. You can use an electric fan with it. 獨自在家時，一次只可開一部冷氣，可同時使用風扇。
  3. Close all the windows and doors when the air conditioner is on. 開冷氣時關閉所有門窗。
  4. No television when you are alone at home with the baby. You may not hear the baby cry. 獨自與寶寶在家時不可看電視，以免聽不到寶寶哭。
  5. Switch off unnecessary electrical appliances when you are going out. 外出時關掉不必要的電器。

F. Home Hygiene and Tidiness 家居衞生及整潔

  1. Open all the windows to a small gap when the weather permits; close them for the night or when leaving the house. 天氣許可時，把所有窗打開一小縫；晚上或外出時關窗。
  2. Open all the curtains at daytime to allow sunshine. 日間打開所有窗簾讓陽光進入。
  3. Keep the kitchen and loo clean and dry at all times. 隨時保持廚房及洗手間清潔乾爽。
  4. Manually vacuum and mop the floor and scrub the bath every other day. Refill the robot water tank every day. Change the robot's mop sheet every 2 weeks. 每兩天人手吸塵、拖地及刷浴缸。每天為掃地機械人加水。每兩星期更換機械人的拖布。
  5. Ventilate (HI mode) the bathroom for 1 hour after use. 洗手間使用後開抽氣扇（HI 強檔）1 小時。
  6. Always maintain a high level of personal hygiene. Wash hands with soap when returning home from outside. 時刻保持高度個人衞生。從外面回家後用肥皂洗手。
