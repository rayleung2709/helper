C. Baby Care

  1. Wear clean, comfortable clothing suitable for child care. Wash hands with soap before caring for the baby.
  2. Ensure all feeding equipment is sterilised before each use.
  3. Monitor the baby for any food allergies or indigestion.
  4. Keep the baby's bed safe and clean. Make sure the baby is sleeping on his back.
  5. Change diapers promptly to prevent rashes and discomfort. Dispose of diapers in a sanitary manner.
  6. Bathe the baby daily with baby-safe products. Never leave the baby unattended during bath.
  7. Monitor the baby's health and report any symptoms of illness immediately. Give medication only as directed by us.
  8. Keep the environment free from hazards (e.g. sharp objects, small items that can be swallowed).

D. Cellphone

  1. Minimise the use of cellphone, especially if you are the only adult with the baby, no matter inside or outside the house.
  2. If you need extra mobile data, you need to buy it from your own pocket.
  3. You can use our home WiFi after finishing your work.

E. Electrical Appliances

  1. Air conditioners may be used when it is higher than 24°C, or if you have a pretty good reason.
  2. Only turn on one air conditioner at a time when you are alone at home. You can use an electric fan with it.
  3. Close all the windows and doors when the air conditioner is on.
  4. No television when you are alone at home with the baby. You may not hear the baby cry.
  5. Switch off unnecessary electrical appliances when you are going out.

F. Home Hygiene and Tidiness

  1. Open all the windows to a small gap when the weather permits; close them for the night or when leaving the house.
  2. Open all the curtains at daytime to allow sunshine.
  3. Keep the kitchen and loo clean and dry at all times.
  4. Manually vacuum and mop the floor and scrub the bath every other day. Refill the robot water tank every day. Change the robot's mop sheet every 2 weeks.
  5. Ventilate (HI mode) the bathroom for 1 hour after use.
  6. Always maintain a high level of personal hygiene. Wash hands with soap when returning home from outside.
